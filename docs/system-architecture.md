## 2. WORKFLOW MAP — END TO END

```
SUBSCRIBER JOURNEY
──────────────────
[1] Person visits shoplovelaw.com or landing page
        ↓
[2] Selects plan (individual or institution)
        ↓
[3] Pays via Shopify → webhook or form triggers GHL
        ↓
[4] GHL contact created → auto-synced to Base44 Subscriber entity
        ↓
[5] Subscriber tagged: segment + focus_track + identity_track
        ↓
[6] GHL Workflow fires Day 1 message at 8AM
        ↓
[7] Each day GHL advances the drip — Day 2, Day 3... Day 90
        ↓
[8] Subscriber replies → GHL two-way SMS handles distress signals
        ↓
[9] Day 90 → subscriber offered renewal or upgrade


CONTENT PIPELINE
────────────────
[1] Lex generates 90-day batch (30 days in advance minimum)
        ↓
[2] Messages land in Base44 as "pending"
        ↓
[3] Dominique reviews in Approval Dashboard
        ↓
[4] Approved → status = "approved"
        ↓
[5] Sync function pushes approved messages to GHL as workflow steps
        ↓
[6] GHL owns the send schedule — no daily Base44 automation needed


INSTITUTIONAL FLOW (NEW)
────────────────────────
[1] Law school / bar prep company / firm contacts Love Law
        ↓
[2] Enterprise form / sales call → custom plan quoted
        ↓
[3] Institution provides student/staff list (CSV or form)
        ↓
[4] Lex bulk-imports subscribers with institution tag
        ↓
[5] Assigned to correct segment + focus_track
        ↓
[6] Same daily drip — GHL handles delivery
        ↓
[7] Institution admin gets monthly report (generated by Lex)
```

## 4. SWOT ANALYSIS

### STRENGTHS
- **No-code, fully automated** — content flows from AI to approval to delivery with minimal manual work
- **Deeply segmented** — 6 audience segments × 14 focus tracks = hyper-personalized content at scale
- **Faith-based track** — differentiator in a market that largely ignores this audience
- **Dual market** — individual subscribers AND institutional contracts = multiple revenue streams
- **GSM-7 compliance** — cost-optimized SMS delivery from day one
- **Existing brand** — shoplovelaw.com has real traffic, pricing, and SMS opt-in already live
- **Two-way SMS** — distress response logic builds trust and retention

### WEAKNESSES
- **Content approval bottleneck** — all 519 messages still pending; delivery cannot start until approved
- **No landing pages yet** — no dedicated subscribe funnel outside the Shopify store
- **GHL drip build pending** — messages are approved in Base44 but not yet loaded into GHL workflows
- **Faith-based track not generated yet** — a promised differentiator with no content
- **Institutional sales process undefined** — no form, pitch deck, or onboarding flow yet
- **Single language** — no non-English content yet despite the architecture supporting it

### OPPORTUNITIES
- **Law schools** — thousands of 1Ls starting every August; high-value institutional contracts
- **Bar prep companies** — Themis, Barbri, BarMax could white-label or partner
- **Faith-based legal community** — virtually no competitor serving this intersection
- **Corporate law firms** — attorney wellness programs are growing; DDJ fits this need
- **Multicultural tracks** — Black, Latino, Asian American, LGBTQ+ legal professionals = underserved
- **Merchandise cross-sell** — Shopify store already exists; daily messages drive brand loyalty and purchases
- **Referral engine** — law students talk; one satisfied subscriber = multiple referrals in their section

### THREATS
- **SMS compliance changes** — A2P 10DLC rules are tightening; carrier filtering is a real risk
- **GHL platform dependency** — if GHL changes pricing or API access, delivery is disrupted
- **Credit exhaustion** — if subscriber growth outpaces Base44 plan upgrades, automation breaks
- **Content fatigue** — 90-day sequences need refreshing; stale content = churn
- **Copycats** — the concept is simple enough to replicate once visible in the market
- **Bar exam seasonality** — bar prep segment is highly seasonal (Feb/July exams); revenue will spike and dip
